Who we are

Lina Expo Exhibitions and Conferences is a Sudanese institution founded in Khartoum in 2015. It is a business name of ISM, a Sudanese national company established in 2004. The company has experience in planning and running local exhibitions and in coordinating and managing Sudanese participation in regional and international exhibitions, helping promote Sudanese products and services, open new markets and strengthen trade and investment relations. It is a member of the Sudanese Exhibition Organizers Chamber and operates under the supervision of the Sudanese Ministry of Trade within the applicable regulatory frameworks.

Our vision and mission

Our vision
To be one of Sudan's leading institutions in the exhibitions and conferences industry, and an effective bridge linking Sudanese products, projects and investment opportunities to regional and global markets.
Our mission
To provide professional, integrated services in organizing exhibitions, conferences and economic and trade events, through innovative planning, effective management and strategic partnerships, strengthening marketing, investment and trade exchange and supporting the national economy.
Our strategic objectives
  • Developing the exhibitions and conferences industry in Sudan.
  • Supporting local producers and small and medium enterprises.
  • Promoting Sudanese industries and products in foreign markets.
  • Organizing national participation in international exhibitions and forums.
  • Strengthening partnerships between Sudanese institutions and regional and international counterparts.
  • Attracting investment, trade and technology opportunities to Sudan.
